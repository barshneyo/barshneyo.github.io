ROGSCO

Resilient Online Graph-based Supply Chain Optimization

Routing petroleum deliveries from depots to fuel stations on real city road networks, while planning for bottlenecks, structural failures and supply-demand shocks. The network is clustered, risk is learned over time, and trucks are routed inside each cluster. A feedback trigger re-runs the clustering and routing when something changes.

Tier 1 results · Transportation Bottleneck · Philadelphia · 30 depots, 160 fuel stations

CGRGC

Congestion-Guided Residual Graph Clustering

A clustering algorithm for multi-drone delivery routing that separates congested areas before routes are planned. Its ideas later evolved into DGKCGC, the first stage of ROGSCO.
